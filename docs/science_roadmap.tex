% ================================================================= section 1
\section{Adopt into the core program}

\subsection{Lock-step spectral tiling}
\cflag{FLAGSHIP}\ \ctier{TIER A}\ \cniche{COMPETITIVE NICHE}

\medskip\noindent
Stop sitting at one mass point per magnet. Step the \dnmr{} lock offset through
${\sim}27$ positions a night and stitch in absolute-frequency space: the
$\pm$few-kHz lock shift multiplies by $\gamma_{\mathrm H}/\gamma_{\mathrm D} = 6.51$
at the \hnmr{} channel, so each site tiles \textbf{$\pm$13--26\,kHz of contiguous
mass coverage} instead of an isolated 300\,Hz sliver. The step pattern is also a
veto no fixed-frequency haloscope has: spin lines move with the step, instrument
lines do not, and a real dark-matter line stays put in absolute frequency while
its amplitude follows the resonant envelope.

\begin{numbers}\small\ttfamily
27 bins/night costs $27^{1/4}=2.3\times$ per-bin depth for $27\times$ coverage ---
scan figure-of-merit exactly neutral. OCXO $10^{-7}$--$10^{-8}$ = 6--60\,Hz at
600\,MHz, already adequate vs the 300\,Hz virial width. Lock settle + T1:
seconds vs ${\sim}20$\,min segments.
\end{numbers}

\needs{per-step receiver-carrier follow, per-segment lock-offset metadata in the
bundle, stitching in analysis. A direct upgrade of the shipped v0.5 sweep feature.}

\subsection{Cross-site mass-point overlap and veto protocol}
\ctier{TIER A}\ \cniche{COMPETITIVE NICHE}

\medskip\noindent
Coincidence bookkeeping across sites suppresses accidental line candidates by
$10^{2}$--$10^{4}$. Review corrected two assumptions: the console OCXO already
supports cross-site mass matching to $\pm$one virial linewidth (a GPSDO is
optional hardening, not a prerequisite), and only site pairs at the bottom of the
same-nominal-field spread (60--600\,kHz at 600\,MHz, vs $\pm$13--26\,kHz stepping
reach) can be brought onto the same mass point.

\smallskip
\needs{lock-offset metadata, a cross-site persistent-line catalog,
absolute-frequency bookkeeping discipline in the pipeline.}

% ================================================================= section 2
\section{Free analysis riders --- same recorded bits}

\noindent Four searches that ride the exact bytes the network already uploads.
Zero observing time, zero hardware.

\subsection{Axial-vector dark matter reinterpretation}
\ctier{TIER A}\ \cworld{WORLD-LEADING}

\medskip\noindent
The single best find of the pass. A light vector with a direct axial spin
coupling (a ``dark $Z'$'') drives the \emph{identical} candidate lines as the
axion wind, but without the $v\sim10^{-3}$ velocity suppression. Every core
limit converts by one factor.

\begin{numbers}\small
$g_A = g_{aNN}\, m_a\, v$ \;$\Rightarrow$\; core reach
$g_{aNN}\sim10^{-8}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ gives
$\mathbf{g_A \sim 2.5\e{-26}}$ at 2.5\,\ueV. No direct laboratory limit exists
anywhere in the \ueV{} decade; this sits 4--8 orders below even the indirect
meson-decay bounds.
\end{numbers}

\needs{one coupling-conversion factor and a modified daily-modulation template
in the analysis code. Frame as a limit --- UV completions of light
non-conserved axial vectors are contrived, so never as a discovery favorite.}

\subsection{In-bore dark-photon line search}
\ctier{TIER A}\ \cniche{COMPETITIVE NICHE}

\medskip\noindent
Dark-photon dark matter drives an effective current inside the detection volume;
the below-cutoff bore blocks external RFI but not this in-volume source, so
every probe is a small shielded haloscope. Review corrected the first-pass power
estimate by ${\sim}300\times$ (a sub-wavelength shielded pickup pays
$(R/\lambdabar)^2\approx0.016$ at 600\,MHz) and shrank the ``virgin territory''
claim after checking historical coverage: original ADMX scanned 460--890\,MHz in
2010, CAPP-12TB covered 1.025--1.185\,GHz.

\begin{numbers}\small
$\varepsilon\sim1.4\e{-11}$ per cryoprobe-night,
$\mathbf{{\sim}3\e{-12}}$ \textbf{per cryo site-year}: wins 3--30$\times$ over
WISPDMX (${\sim}10^{-11}$) and cosmology (${\sim}10^{-10}$) in the genuinely
unscanned windows --- ${\sim}$400--460\,MHz (the 9.4\,T sites) plus gaps between
historical scans.
\end{numbers}

\needs{console spurs are \emph{also} immobile under lock stepping (the lock
moves $B_0$, not the synthesizer) --- spur discrimination rests on the
clock-audit-keyed spur catalog, blank-tube nights, and optionally a GPSDO
(tightens the absolute-frequency test from ${\sim}60$\,Hz to $<1$\,Hz).}

\subsection{High-resolution sub-virial channel}
\ctier{TIER A}\ \cniche{COMPETITIVE NICHE}

\medskip\noindent
A second FFT pass at ${\sim}0.1$\,Hz resolution buys 2.5--7$\times$ in coupling
on cold-flow and caustic substructure --- ADMX runs the same high-resolution
trick for the photon coupling; nobody does it for the gradient coupling at these
mass points. Non-negotiable detail from review: Earth's rotation against a
${\sim}300$\,km/s flow chirps the line by ${\sim}0.9$\,Hz over a night, more
than its width, so \emph{diurnal} chirp templates are mandatory, not just annual
ones.

\smallskip
\needs{second FFT pass, diurnal+annual template bank, lock-step amplitude veto
against the sub-Hz instrumental spur forest. OCXO stability floors usable
linewidth at ${\sim}0.06$\,Hz.}

\subsection{Calibrated excess-power archive (public data product)}
\ctier{TIER A}\ \cfirst{FIRST-LIMIT TERRITORY}

\medskip\noindent
A versioned public release: calibrated PSDs + covariance + spur list + measured
coil/shield transfer function (including the sub-Compton shielding suppression)
+ field, orientation, coordinates per site. On every model anyone has written
down we lose 1--3 orders to dedicated experiments --- but the archive makes the
dark-photon, axion--photon, and high-frequency gravitational-wave
reinterpretation tables free forever, and in our exact slivers it is the only
limit of any kind.

% ================================================================= section 3
\section{Worth a protocol change (Tier B)}

\subsection{Sidereal Lorentz violation on the clock-blind \hnmr/\dnmr{} ratio}
\ctier{TIER A/B}\ \cworld{WORLD-LEADING}

\medskip\noindent
Both frequencies come from the same OCXO, so oscillator and $B_0$ cancel exactly
in the ratio --- and the lock servo is the mechanism, not a bug: it transduces
any deuteron-sector shift into $B_0$ and hence into the \hnmr{} channel. The
\emph{B-scaled} channel (an anisotropy of the bound proton/deuteron magnetic
moment, $\delta g \propto B$) is virgin territory that high field actively helps.

\begin{numbers}\small
Statistical: ${\sim}2\e{-13}$ per network-year. Systematics haircut (spin-noise
line pull needs a demonstrated $10^{2}$--$10^{3}$ sidereal-coherent
suppression): floor $\mathbf{3\e{-12}}$--$\mathbf{10^{-11}}$ fractional ---
still beats BASE's proton sidereal sensitivity (${\sim}10^{-9}$ at 1.9\,T) by
\textbf{100--1000$\times$}, with $\times$7--15 extra coefficient reach at
14--28\,T. Conceded: the B-independent $\tilde b$ channel loses $10^{4}$--$3\e{7}$
to comagnetometers --- dead, see the ledger.
\end{numbers}

\needs{standardized 90/10 H$_2$O/D$_2$O network sample, per-block lock
error-signal logging, phased-lineshape co-fit, detuning-ladder validation
(already planned for core), an SME theory workup for the deuteron.}

\subsection{Isotope-ladder X-nuclei campaign --- \fnmr{} adopted}
\ctier{TIER B}\ \cfirst{FIRST-LIMIT TERRITORY}\ \cflag{\textsuperscript{19}F ADOPTED}

\medskip\noindent
\dnmr{} and \cnmr{} nights give the first \emph{laboratory} limits
on the axion--neutron coupling at these mass points (\pnmr{} is
odd-proton --- a proton-coupling mass point on the X channel, not a
neutron probe); \fnmr{} at $0.941\times$
the \hnmr{} frequency rides the \hnmr/\fnmr{} coil at near-cryo sensitivity ---
essentially free proton mass points densifying the scan. Review killed a wrong
$\gamma^{1.5}$ scaling (the X channel is circuit-noise-limited; real numbers are
10--30$\times$ worse than first proposed) and the campaign survives as
lab-frontier exclusion, honestly above SN1987A.

\begin{numbers}\small
$g_{ann}\sim2\e{-5}$--$2\e{-4}$ per season per mass point via \dnmr.
Protocol rules that survived: \dnmr{} observation \textbf{requires lock-off
nights} (the lock transmitter irradiates the deuterium line; drift 0.1\,Hz/hr is
negligible vs the 46\,Hz virial width); \cnmr{} needs overnight decoupling or
$^{13}$CS$_2$ against the 140\,Hz J-quartet.
\end{numbers}

\noindent\textbf{Decision, 2026-09-01:} the \fnmr{} rider is \textbf{adopted}
into the near-term program, split off from the rest of the ladder --- it needs
no X-channel calibration work, a C$_6$F$_6$ tube is cheap and inert, and every
F-capable site that runs it densifies the proton mass scan for free. The \dnmr/\cnmr{} neutron-coupling legs (and the \pnmr{}
proton-channel leg) stay Tier B pending volunteer sites.

\smallskip
\needs{ATMA retune scripts, per-nucleus calibration, ${\sim}\$100$--500 in
samples (neat D$_2$O, \cnmr-methanol or $^{13}$CS$_2$, C$_6$F$_6$, 85\%
H$_3$PO$_4$) --- all standard 5\,mm tubes.}

\subsection{Dual-species transient network (GNOME complement)}
\ctier{TIER B}\ \cniche{COMPETITIVE NICHE}

\medskip\noindent
Interleaved ${\sim}1^\circ$ FIDs at 0.5--1\,Hz on a ${\sim}1\%$ H$_2$O / 99\%
D$_2$O sample track the \hnmr{} line at ${\sim}1$\,mHz/s --- 23\,pT
proton-equivalent, the same decade as GNOME's effective \emph{proton}-coupling
floor (alkali magnetometers pay ${\sim}\mu_N/\mu_B$ against nuclear couplings)
--- with exact $\gamma_d/\gamma_p$ dual-species tagging for domain-wall
candidates. The systematic that sets the dilution: at 10\% H$_2$O, radiation
damping pulls the line at $1000\times$ the target; at 1\% it is controllable,
pending demonstration.

\smallskip
\needs{$\geq$10\,Hz lock-error logging, the interleaved-FID pulse program, lock
transfer-function calibration via existing lock-shift stepping, a GNOME-style
coincidence pipeline on NTP timestamps.}

\subsection{Foundational pair: FDT at GHz and collective-decay metrology}
\ctier{TIER A/B}\ \cniche{COMPETITIVE NICHE}

\medskip\noindent
\textbf{(a)} First quantitative fluctuation--dissipation test on a nuclear-spin
ensemble at GHz: calibrated spin-noise PSD vs small-flip $\chi''$ at the
${\sim}1\%$ absolute-calibration floor, including the two-temperature cryoprobe
case (20\,K coil, 300\,K spins). Verified unoccupied territory. The
quantum-asymmetry stretch goal is dead: a phase-insensitive amplifier measures
the symmetrized spectrum, correction $(hf/2kT)^2/3\sim2\e{-7}$.
\textbf{(b)} Absolute radiation-damping metrology: the $\omega Q\eta M_0$
collective-decay rate tested at few-percent absolute across $2\times$ in
frequency and $100\times$ in $M_0$ on many platforms --- ${\sim}10\times$ beyond
the single-instrument literature. Frame as coupled-mode physics, not
``Purcell,'' or referees will object.

% ================================================================= section 4
\section{Gated or deferred}

\subsection{Axion--photon piggyback --- gated on one number}
\cfirst{GATED}\ \ctier{TIER A + 1 WK SIM}

\medskip\noindent
The 9.4--28\,T field through the detection volume makes every probe a (terrible)
haloscope --- but an NMR coil is engineered to have zero leading-order overlap
with the axisymmetric conversion field, and the residual form factor $C$ (lead
routing, tilts, dielectric asymmetries) is unknown between $10^{-2}$ and
$10^{-5}$. The gate is ${\sim}1$ week of full-wave EM simulation of the 2--3
dominant commercial probe geometries.

\begin{numbers}\small
$C\geq10^{-2}$: $g\sim2\e{-11}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ per cryo-site-year ---
\textbf{beats CAST/globular clusters ($6.6\e{-11}$/$4.7\e{-11}$) by
${\sim}3\times$} in the 1.65--2.7\,\ueV{} window no cavity experiment has ever
scanned. $C\sim10^{-3}$: a tie --- publish the simulation and stop. Claim
nothing until $C$ is in hand.
\end{numbers}

\subsection{Idle-MRI fleet nodes (64--298\,MHz)}
\cfirst{PILOT FIRST}\ \ctier{TIER B, NEW INSTRUMENTS}

\medskip\noindent
The 10\,L volume win survives honest accounting of coil dilution and room
temperature: ${\sim}5\times$ one cryoprobe-night per scanner-night in amplitude
SNR, at 0.26--0.53\,\ueV{} mass points nothing else covers. A fleet-year reaches
few${}\e{-8}$--$10^{-7}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ --- the closest approach to the
SN1987A bound anywhere in the program. Real costs are institutional: raw-FID
console access, a ${\sim}10$\,L phantom, per-scanner characterization. Pilot on
2--3 research scanners before any fleet claim.

\subsection{Also parked}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item \textbf{CASPEr-electric-lite} (poled ferroelectric $^{207}$Pb stick,
${\sim}\$100$--500): arithmetic survives, but disorder broadening in relaxors
costs another 3--10$\times$; pilot-grade, non-cryo probe first.
\item \textbf{Broadband X-channel scan}: the $(R/\lambdabar)^2$ suppression is
fatal below ${\sim}300$\,MHz (loses 100--1000$\times$ to Dark E-field Radio);
only the 300--460\,MHz corner on $\geq$23.5\,T systems survives, and
${\sim}0.5$\,TB/night storage dominates the cost.
\item \textbf{GPSDO clock-defect search}: scientifically near-empty (at
transient timescales a GPSDO output is its own local quartz) --- but buy the
${\sim}\$300$--1000 GPSDO anyway for spur discrimination and cross-night
stitching.
\item \textbf{Driven-lock nonequilibrium FDT}: real physics, but the \dnmr{}
noise feature is ${\sim}10^{-2}$ of the \hnmr{} bump; needs raw lock-receiver
access. Park it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Considered and killed --- the ledger}

\noindent Every rejection was independently re-derived before being recorded.
The ledger exists so these are never re-proposed.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=5pt]
\item \textbf{ULF dark photon via the lock / persistent-current channel}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small lose by $>$13 orders to SuperMAG}\\
{\small A sub-wavelength pickup pays $(L/\lambdabar)$ in field: $6\e{-12}$ at
mHz. SuperMAG's Earth-radius loop has a $2\e{7}$ head start no lab apparatus
buys back.}
\item \textbf{Monopole--dipole force with an in-bore source mass}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small lose by 7--8 orders to $^3$He/$^{129}$Xe}\\
{\small 0.5\,kg at 10\,cm reaches $g_sg_p\sim10^{-22}$ vs comagnetometer bounds
at $10^{-29}$--$10^{-30}$. Geometry cannot rescue it.}
\item \textbf{B-independent sidereal $\tilde b$ on the \hnmr/\dnmr{} ratio}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small lose by $10^{4}$--$3\e{7}$}\\
{\small Systematic floor $2.5\e{-26}$\,GeV vs bounds down to $8.4\e{-34}$. The
deuteron-combination escape fails: $\mu_d\approx\mu_p+\mu_n$, triangle
inequality closes it. Only the B-scaled channel lives.}
\item \textbf{Hz--kHz stochastic ALP sidebands / distant-site cross-correlation}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small lose by 2--6 orders}\\
{\small Best case $g_p\sim10^{-4}\,\mathrm{GeV}^{-1}$ vs NASDUCK-class. And the
canonical answer to ``why not cross-correlate distant sites?'': the DM coherence
length at \ueV{} masses is ${\sim}500$\,m.}
\item \textbf{Astrophysical burst follow-up (SN axions, FRBs, solar)}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small in-band weight $\leq10^{-27}$}\\
{\small MeV supernova axions oscillate at $10^{22}$\,Hz; in-band FRB EM is
attenuated $e^{-35}$ by the below-cutoff bore and loses to thermal noise even
unshielded.}
\item \textbf{Pauli-exclusion violation in NMR}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small lose by $\geq10^{9}$}\\
{\small Wrong-symmetry admixtures in liquids produce no new frequencies ---
only an intensity shift degenerate with concentration. Best conceivable bound
${\sim}10^{-2}$ vs spectroscopy at $10^{-11}$.}
\item \textbf{Millicharged-particle cyclotron lines}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small conditions mutually exclusive}\\
{\small In-band cyclotron and terrestrial capture cannot both hold: they miss
each other by $>$an order of magnitude in $\varepsilon$ at every mass.}
\item \textbf{Oscillating quark masses (dilaton DM) in the ratio}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small MICROSCOPE wins by $\geq$3.5 orders}\\
{\small Best-case $d_{m_q}\sim1.6$ at the single most favorable mass. The ratio
time series survives as a free drift diagnostic only.}
\item \textbf{High-frequency gravitational waves as a physics driver}
\hfill \textcolor{killink}{\small $h\sim6\e{-18}$, sources $<10^{-27}$}\\
{\small Survives only as a free reinterpretation table in the excess-power
archive.}
\end{itemize}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=numbg,colframe=numbg,arc=2pt,left=8pt,right=8pt]
\textbf{Three rules the kills taught us}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Sub-wavelength EM pickups pay $(L/\lambdabar)$ in field} --- an
$O(0.1)$ penalty at 600\,MHz, a death sentence at mHz. Low-frequency ambitions
belong to spin-coupled channels.
\item \textbf{The \ueV{} dark-matter field is coherent over ${\sim}500$\,m} ---
same-hall pairs are phase-coherent; the worldwide network is not. Its power is
mass points and vetoes, not coherent combining.
\item \textbf{Phase-insensitive amplifiers measure the symmetrized spectrum}
--- linear-in-$hf/kT$ quantum asymmetries are invisible; the accessible
correction is $(hf/2kT)^2/3$.
\end{itemize}
\end{tcolorbox}
